## What's new
- [Learning the Context of Errors: Black-Box Online Adaptation of TSFMs (ORCA)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14222) (Dai et al., Ant International / CUHK, Jun 2026) — online residual adapter for a frozen, closed-API TSFM: learns from the base model's errors conditioned on its inputs *and* outputs, no weights or gradients needed. Reports gains across 5 TSFMs / 8 datasets (a third-party summary says up to ~21% error reduction; unverified). Code: Fifthky/ORCA.
- [Unified Zero-Shot Time Series Forecasting: A Darts Foundation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27438) (Dai, Bader, Gysi, Jun 2026) — Darts gains a unified `FoundationModel` interface over Chronos-2, TimesFM 2.5, TiRex, PatchTST-FM. Tooling, not a method; noted in case a second TSFM is wanted in the harness.
- [Excitation paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06447) — already used in Post 02; arXiv ID (2610.06447, 5 Oct 2026) recorded for reference.
