## What's new
- [Forecast Collapse in Time-Series Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.14106) (Wan, Ma et al., Aug 2026) — on hourly returns for ~1,000 US stocks, TSFM forecasts go nearly flat and rank stocks poorly; effect largely absent for trading volume. Ties it to target predictability; proposes CalibRank (calibration + ranking objective).
- [t0: A Time-Series Foundation Model for Forecasting with Context](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.24559) (The Forecasting Company, Sep 2026) — open-weights t0-alpha (102M) / t0-beta (256M), conditioned on past and known-future covariates; reports ~6.3 pts of skill from known-future covariates across 30 tasks, 3rd on GIFT-Eval (CRPS 0.4738).
- [Efficient Test-Time Scaling for LLM-based Time Series Forecasting (SCALER)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.08675) (Le et al., KDD 2026 oral) — lightweight Transformer predicts coarse shape; LLM refines via a few coarse-to-fine residual-token steps. Claims better accuracy at lower inference cost.
- [TemporalBench](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3770855.3817485) (Aug 2026) — benchmark for LLM agents on context- and event-informed time-series tasks. Skim only.
- [Frontiers in AI: hybrid residual-learning forecast for pharmaceutical demand](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/artificial-intelligence/articles/10.3389/frai.2026.1803863/full) — SVR base + DNN residual correction; applied, single dataset. Skim only.
- [Springer chapter: ARIMA + LSTM residual hybrid for agricultural prices](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-981-95-7289-2_19) — authors say hybrid benefit depends on residual variability; consistent with the "when does it earn its place" framing.

## Candidate topics this week
- **Does Chronos-2 "collapse" to a flat forecast on SPY?** — source: [Forecast Collapse](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.14106). Why: SPY is already the low-signal series; a small demo can measure forecast dispersion/amplitude and directional accuracy vs naive and ARIMA under the existing harness (only the single-series analogue, not the cross-sectional ranking claim).
- **Known-future covariates: pretrained covariate use vs. hand-built Fourier/calendar features on CISO** — source: [t0](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.24559). Why: pairs naturally with Demo 3; asks whether a TSFM using covariates beats a lag+Fourier learner, at what runtime cost.
- **Cheap shape model + expensive refiner: coarse-to-fine hybrids** — source: [SCALER](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.08675). Why: a seasonal-naive/Fourier "shape" plus a Chronos or residual-memory correction is a small analogue; accuracy vs compute is the story.
