## What's new
- [Evaluating TSFMs for Electricity Price Forecasting: Contamination Risk, Distributional Shifts, and Covariate Dependence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02623) (Pan & Ezzat, ICML 2026 workshop; surfaced in the Oct 7 listings) — two-dataset benchmarking to limit pretraining contamination. TSFMs are competitive with general baselines but depend heavily on covariate support and don't reliably beat domain-specific methods; combining the two looks promising. Close to this repo's "pretrained vs. locally demonstrated value" question.
- [Horizon-Constrained Rashomon Sets for Chaotic Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05218) — models tied on short-horizon accuracy can diverge at long horizons (wind power example). Older (May), but it is the verified background for the new [Temporal Predictive Multiplicity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09994) *(title only)* preprint (Oct 7).
- [Training on the Future: A Delay-Aware Audit of Test-Time Adaptation for Time-Series Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12232) *(title only)* — Oct 8. Title suggests it audits whether TTA methods use targets that wouldn't yet be observed at forecast time. Related TTA background: [TAFAS](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.04970v1).
- Retrieval + foundation models: [SteerCast](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11229) *(title only)* (retrieval-based latent steering for decoder-only forecasters) and [ROAR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24911) *(title only)* (retrieval-opportunity-aware refinement, zero-shot). Same family as Demo 2's residual memory.
- [CARE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11165) *(title only)* — lightweight plug-in gated correction + uncertainty module for long-term forecasting; another post-hoc correction layer.
- [CTRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23257) v2 landed Oct 8 (already tracked in literature/).
- [OpenTSLM TeeMoE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40265) *(title only)* — unified time-series language model; code/weights on [GitHub](https://github.com/OpenTSLM/OpenTSLM-TeeMoE). Release noted, no clear small demo.
- Quiet on hybrid classical+ML: nothing new and substantive beyond the above this week.

## Candidate topics this week
- **Do "equally accurate" models forecast the same trajectory?** — source: [Rashomon sets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05218), [Temporal Predictive Multiplicity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09994). Why: with the existing harness, compare seasonal-naive/ARIMA/Chronos/hybrid forecasts that tie on MASE and measure how much their paths disagree by horizon; cheap and fits the "report what happened" stance.
- **Delay-aware test-time adaptation: what can you update on, and when?** — source: [Training on the Future](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12232) (title only), [TAFAS](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.04970v1). Why: a causal online correction to a frozen forecast that only uses targets revealed by the origin is a small demo, with a leaky-vs-causal comparison as the honest-evaluation point.
- **Contamination and covariates: how much of a zero-shot win is real?** — source: [2607.02623](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02623). Why: re-run Demo 1's zero-shot vs. local comparison with/without covariates (temperature, calendar) on CISO and on a period unlikely to be in pretraining data.
- **Retrieval at the input vs. retrieval at the residual** — source: [SteerCast](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11229), [ROAR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24911) (both title only). Why: contrast Demo 2's residual retrieval with retrieving similar windows as context for a foundation model; verify abstracts before committing.
